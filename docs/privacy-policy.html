Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

What we collect

When you connect your QuickBooks account to Anton, we store encrypted access and refresh tokens that allow the software to read and write your QuickBooks data on your behalf. We do not store your Intuit username or password.

How we use your data

  • Tokens are used solely to interact with the QuickBooks company you authorized \u2014 reading transactions, creating invoices, and similar bookkeeping operations.
  • All money-movement and outbound actions require explicit human approval before execution.
  • Tokens are stored AES-GCM encrypted in a local credential broker on your own server. They never leave your infrastructure except to call Intuit's API.

What we don't do

  • We never sell, share, or transmit your QuickBooks data to third parties.
  • We never use your data to train AI models.
  • We never store your Intuit login credentials.

Data retention

Tokens persist until you disconnect the app or revoke access via your QuickBooks settings. Disconnecting immediately revokes all stored tokens.
